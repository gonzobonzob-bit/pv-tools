How to read a flag & when to dispatch

What "bleed" measures: k = how much reported usage rises per watt of production. k = 0.48 means for every 100 W the array produces, reported usage rises 48 W — usually a consumption CT clamped on the wrong conductor, not a real electrical fault.

strong / moderate — clear slope, most or many days affected. Compare the reported figure against the customer's utility bill first; if the bill confirms the gap, it's CT placement and dispatch is justified. If the bill matches reported usage, there's a real daytime load and nothing here is a metering fault.

intermittent — some days flagged, others not. Wiring does not come and go, so this is usually not a wiring fault — ask about a pool pump, EV charger, or timed HVAC before doing anything else.

Before booking a truck: (1) confirm the consumption CT is configured whole-home in the portal, not on a PV backfeed branch — a portal check, if that field is exposed to you; (2) compare against the bill — this is the check that actually settles it; (3) only then dispatch.

Confidence: the strong/moderate threshold rests on 4 systems, 16 days of confirmed outcomes — a real lead, not a settled verdict. A genuinely solar-timed load can produce a similar slope. Say this plainly if anyone asks how sure the tool is.
